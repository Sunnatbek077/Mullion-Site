Privacy

The short version: Mullion itself uploads nothing. The longer version is below, including the one place your sign-in details do leave your Mac — when you sign in to a store.

⌀

Nothing is uploaded

Mullion uploads nothing — not your library, not your game names, not a usage ping.

↗

One request that isn’t a download

The app makes exactly one outbound request that is not a download: a HEAD to read a component's size.

A HEAD request asks a server how big a file is without fetching the file itself.

!

The store tools are a different matter

The store tools it runs are a different matter: Steam, Epic and GOG each receive your credentials, because signing in to a store means signing in to that store.

That is between you and the store — the same as signing in on its own website.

⌂

Everything stays on your Mac

Everything Mullion writes stays in ~/Mullion.
